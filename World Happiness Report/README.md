# 🌍 World Happiness Report Dashboard (Power BI)
## 🔎 Overview
The World Happiness Report Dashboard (Power BI) provides interactive insights into global happiness using factors like GDP per capita, social support, and corruption. It allows users to compare countries, analyze top & bottom rankings, and explore the correlation between economic and social indicators with overall happiness.

This interactive dashboard helps users explore the top and bottom-ranking countries, correlations between GDP and happiness, and regional distributions in a clean and dynamic interface.

## 🎯 Objective

- To visualize and compare happiness scores of countries worldwide.
- To identify factors influencing happiness such as GDP, social support, and corruption.
- To provide interactive exploration using filters and slicers.
- To support policy-making and research through data-driven insights.


## ✨ Key Features
### ✅ KPI Cards – Highlight key insights such as:
- Country with Highest GDP → Qatar (1.68)
- Country with Highest Happiness Score → Finland (7.77)
- Country with Lowest Corruption → Singapore (0.45)

### ✅ Top & Bottom Countries –
- Bar charts for countries with the highest and lowest happiness scores.

### ✅ GDP vs Happiness Score (Scatter Plot) –
- Visual correlation between GDP per capita and overall happiness.

### ✅ Interactive Map Visualization –
- Country-level happiness distribution across the world.

### ✅ Tree Maps –
- GDP per Capita by Country.
- Social Support by Country.

### ✅ Dynamic Filters & Slicers –
- Select by country or region for customized insights.

### ✅ Interactive Table –
- Country-wise Happiness Ranking with total scores.

## 📈 Key Insights
- Top 3 Happiest Countries: Finland (7.77), Denmark (7.60), Norway (7.55).
- Lowest Happiness Scores: Haiti, Botswana, Syria (<4).
- GDP Leaders: Qatar, Luxembourg, Singapore show the highest GDP per capita.
- Social Support: Iceland, Denmark, Finland lead with strong community support.
- A positive correlation exists between GDP per capita and Happiness Scores.
